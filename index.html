How this works

QuietType listens to the rhythm of your typing — the gaps between keys, how long you pause, how often you reach for backspace — and uses it to play a soundscape underneath you. It never reads what you write.

  • Steady typing plays short plucked notes, in key.
  • A pause of a second or two brings a pad in underneath.
  • Backspacing and hesitation layer a detuned texture on top.
  • Ten seconds of quiet and everything falls away but one slow tone.

There is no server. Your note is kept in this browser only, and export builds the file on your machine.
